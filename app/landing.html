What DocScrub does

Review detected items in bulk. Keep, replace, or remove them while preserving the original formatting.

  • Local processing Your private data never leaves your computer.
  • Review once. Apply everywhere. One decision can update related items across one or more documents.
  • Controlled output Nothing changes until you approve the final result.

How it works

  1. Choose one or more related files.
  2. Resolve ambiguous names, acronyms, etc.
  3. Review grouped items in bulk.
  4. Review remaining individual items.
  5. Approve and export sanitized copies.
